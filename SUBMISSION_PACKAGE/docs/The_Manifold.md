# The Manifold
**The geometric foundation of the Sovereign Prime Architecture.**

Project Riemann operates within a 3D Conformal Manifold. Unlike traditional number theory, which treats integers as a linear sequence, we treat them as discrete points in a convergent 3D space.

## Topological Mapping
Every integer $n$ is assigned a unique coordinate $(r, \theta, z)$ in the manifold:

*   **Radial Distance ($r$)**: Defined by $1/\ln(n)$. This represents the "inward pull" of the manifold. As $n$ increases, the radius decreases, pulling the system toward the central singularity.
*   **Angular Position ($\theta$)**: Defined by $2\pi\phi \cdot n$, where $\phi$ is the Golden Ratio. This ensures optimal packing density across the spiral.
*   **Vertical Axis ($z$)**: Represents the imaginary component $t$ of the Zeta zeros, mapping the growth of the system over time.

## The Singularity
The manifold is designed as an **Inward Convergence**. By sighting from the "Infinite Outer Ring" back toward the origin, we can identify primes as the necessary structural support nodes for the entire manifold's volume.
